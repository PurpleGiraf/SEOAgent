# Discovery questionnaire

Source: `assets/PG-MAP-discovery-questions.pdf` — Purple Giraffe's actual
client-facing "Marketing Action Plan – Discovery Questions" document, sent
to a new client at the start of an engagement to gather what can't be
sourced from research alone. Lynda also referenced this in the process
walkthrough as the standard first step before research begins.

## How to use this in the skill

This is the **question set to put to the user/client at intake (Step 1)**,
not something to answer by guessing or inventing. Where the user hasn't
already supplied an answer, ask — don't fabricate mission/vision/values,
sales-by-channel splits, or login access. Where an item genuinely can't be
obtained in this session (e.g. no CRM/EDM login available), mark the
related MAP content "not available — client to provide" rather than
inventing it, consistent with `service-context.md`'s data-sourcing rules.

## The questions, grouped, and where each feeds the MAP

### Business / Organisation
- Mission, Vision, Values → About / Branding and positioning
- Business plan (if the client has one, get it directly — feeds Industry
  research and About far faster/more accurately than research from
  scratch, per `service-context.md`)
- Marketing goals; what they want to achieve in 1–3 years → Business
  objective and commercial alignment, Marketing strategy roadmap
- What does success look like (brand awareness / sales-lead gen / other)
  → Key performance indicators
- Unique Selling Proposition (USP) → Branding and positioning
- Company history, in the owner's own words (even though it can be partly
  read from the website) → About → Company history
- Positioning — where they see themselves in the marketplace → Branding
  and positioning
- Problem the business solves; what they're good at; what they could
  improve on → SWOT analysis, Point of difference

### Industry Data
- Any industry data/reports the client can share → About → Industry
  research and insights, Appendix 1 (use directly if available, faster and
  more reliable than starting research from zero)
- Known/estimated market share → About / Industry research and insights

### Channels to Market
- Channels used (wholesale, retail, direct, export, etc.) and what
  marketing each channel needs → Sales channels and distribution
- Bottlenecks/problems/challenges in the sales funnel → Sales channels and
  distribution actions, Customer journey
- Sales % split by channel → Sales channels and distribution (current vs.
  desired mix)
- Sales % split by product/brand/service offering → Products/services
  portfolio and pricing
- Other channels being targeted (if any) → Sales channel opportunities

### Customer Journey
- Documented customer journey, if one exists → use directly to populate
  the Customer journey touchpoint table instead of inferring it

### Competitors
- Who the client perceives as their major competitors → **this is the
  only acceptable source for the Competitive summary's top 3** — never
  invent or infer competitors independently

### Target Market
- Who they believe their target market is, and the % split across
  segments, now and desired in ~3 years → Target market (6-segment wheel,
  repeated per segment) — the desired split feeds the Strategy for that
  section

### Brand Collateral
- Brand Style Guide, Language/Tone Guide (request copies) → Branding and
  positioning, Tone of voice
- Letterhead, business cards, comp slips, quotes/invoices, email
  signatures, pitch decks/presentations/brochures, social media templates
  → Corporate assets audit

### Digital Media (access, not just information)
- Facebook admin access, other social logins, Google Account admin access,
  Google Analytics access, website login (nice-to-have, not required), EDM
  platform login, CRM details and how it's used → these are the direct
  data sources for the whole Digital media section and Analytics — use
  real exported/observed data wherever access exists, per
  `service-context.md`'s tool list; mark anything inaccessible this
  session as a baseline gap rather than guessing.

### Images
- Existing image library; video content → Images and video section,
  general asset audit throughout

### Policies and Procedure
- Any marketing-relevant policies, regulations, or procedures the
  organisation must follow → carried through as a constraint across
  relevant sections (e.g. regulated industries, ESG/DEI/accessibility
  sections if applicable)

### Existing Relationships / Contacts
- Sponsorships → Sponsorships and associations (conditional section)
- Sensis listing → Traditional advertising
- Traditional advertising activity → Traditional advertising
- Recent brochures/documents/invoice templates (copies) → Corporate
  assets, Traditional advertising audits

## What this changes about Step 1 (Intake)

Previously this skill's intake step asked for a shorter, informally-scoped
list. Use this questionnaire as the actual checklist — it's more complete
and it's the real document Purple Giraffe sends clients. Don't re-ask
questions the user has already answered elsewhere in the conversation, and
don't block progress waiting on items that are genuinely optional
(website login, CRM/EDM access) — proceed and flag the gap instead.
