# Purple Giraffe: real workflow, tools, and constraints

Source: a recorded walkthrough with Lynda Schenk (founder) explaining how
consultants actually build a MAP, plus her instructional comments in the
master template (`section-playbook.md`, `map-structure.md`). This is
grounding for how this skill should behave — not client-facing content.

## Who Purple Giraffe is

Outsourced marketing consultancy, offices in Adelaide (SA), Perth (WA),
Brisbane (QLD). Team spans brand/visual identity, copywriting/PR, paid &
AI/digital, and marketing consulting generalists, plus dedicated graphic
designers.

## The real research/build workflow (in order)

1. **Download the master template** into the client's folder, rename it
   "Marketing action plan – [Client]".
2. **Delete what doesn't apply** before starting — industry-specific
   modules (tourism/accommodation/restaurant/wine club/etc.), channels the
   client doesn't use (no Instagram? delete that block), and optional
   sections (personas, ESG, DEI, accessibility, social-enterprise
   marketing) that don't fit. Delete with tracked changes on, for review.
3. **Industry research** — what's happening in the industry, where does
   this client sit within it. If the client already has a business plan,
   feed it straight in (faster, more accurate than research from scratch).
   If not, budget 2–3+ hours of research — AI has cut this from hours to
   minutes/seconds in practice, but some industries genuinely have little
   available data; when that's the case, don't force an industry-research
   section, lean on competitor information instead.
4. **Competitor research** — the client identifies their competitors (never
   invent them). Review each one's whole public marketing footprint:
   website, social media, findable advertising, targeting, annual reports
   if public. AI has sped this up significantly too.
5. **Company information** — comes from the client (history, direction,
   product/service offering).
6. **Existing marketing audit** — target market segmentation (including the
   "if your brand was a car/animal/city" brand-association exercise),
   traditional marketing audit (brochures, business cards, invoicing,
   print/radio/TV), then digital audit.
7. **Digital audit** — SEMrush for SEO health, broken links, and
   competitor/keyword data; ChatGPT for GEO/AEO analysis (no dedicated tool
   yet); direct review of the website; social media insights and posting
   frequency (via the client's own account access); EDM platform data
   (direct access to the client's Mailchimp/Klaviyo/etc. — most businesses
   don't look at their own data, so this is often where real findings
   surface).
8. **Customer journey** — how does a client actually find them (word of
   mouth, Google, print ad, email, social, PR)? All the way through to
   becoming a customer.
9. **Products/services** — list what's sold; the value isn't the listing
   itself, it's spotting gaps against competitors (e.g. competitors sell
   children's product lines and the client doesn't).
10. **Sales channels** — current channel mix vs. desired mix, since a
    channel shift (e.g. direct-sell → retail) changes the whole marketing
    mix needed to support it.
11. **Synthesis** — from everything above: what are the gaps, what should
    the client start doing, what should they stop doing.
12. **Marketing Action Plan calendar** — the detailed matrix: Marketing
    Element, Activity, Who (Purple Giraffe or the client), Quarter (1–4).
    This is the document's actual deliverable core — everything above
    exists to produce it.

## Tools actually used

- **SEMrush** — the primary tool for SEO audits, meta data, broken-link
  checks, keyword research, competitor SEO/paid-search/social analysis,
  citation "List Management" audits, advertising research. There's an
  internal SEMrush specialist on the team (go-to person for SEMrush
  questions) — if this skill is ever run by a human alongside a real PG
  consultant, defer to them for SEMrush specifics.
- **ChatGPT** (or equivalent LLM) — used for GEO/AEO analysis; no dedicated
  GEO tool exists yet at Purple Giraffe.
- **Google PageSpeed Insights** (free, pagespeed.web.dev) — Core Web
  Vitals, page speed, performance/accessibility/best-practices/SEO scoring.
- **Google Analytics / a digital dashboard** — for the Analytics section.
  A third-party consolidation tool connected via platform APIs is
  recommended over reporting from each social platform individually.
- **Direct platform access** — client's own social media insights, and
  EDM platform (Mailchimp/Klaviyo/etc.) logins, are used directly rather
  than estimated from outside.

## Time and effort (calibrates expected depth)

- Typical MAP: **~50–60 hours** for a straightforward business (limited
  social media, mostly trade-show/offline marketing).
- Complex, multi-entity clients (e.g. an organisation with 7 sub-brands,
  each needing its own website/social review): **120+ hours**.
- First time a consultant writes one, it takes noticeably longer than
  subsequent ones.

## Data sourcing rules (hard constraints for this skill)

- **Competitors come from the client**, never invented or guessed.
- **Industry research**: feed in an existing client business plan if one
  exists; otherwise research directly; if data is genuinely sparse for that
  industry, lean on competitor information instead of forcing weak industry
  content.
- **Digital/social/EDM metrics**: use real platform access/exports where
  available. Where they are not available in a given session, mark KPI
  rows "baseline to be established" — never fabricate numbers.
- **Purple Giraffe's own service pricing** does not appear anywhere in a
  MAP, **except** the "Marketing investment" section, and only when the
  client is continuing on an ongoing PG retainer — see `section-playbook.md`.

## Formatting rule (hard gate, not a suggestion)

Every blue instructional comment/placeholder text in the master template
must be read, actioned, and deleted before a MAP is considered done — none
should remain in a document being delivered to a client. Whole document in
Arial. Images under ~500KB, consistent sizing, JPEG for photos/PNG for
screenshots, kept clear of the footer and the PG giraffe mark.
