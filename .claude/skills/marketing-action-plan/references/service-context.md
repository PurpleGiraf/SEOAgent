# Purple Giraffe: service context, constraints, and known gaps

Background context so recommendations in a generated MAP stay realistic and internally
consistent with what Purple Giraffe can actually deliver. This is not client-facing
content — it's grounding for the agent.

## Who Purple Giraffe is

Outsourced marketing consultancy, offices in Adelaide (SA), Perth (WA), Brisbane (QLD).
Team spans brand/visual identity, copywriting/PR, paid & AI/digital, and marketing
consulting generalists, plus dedicated graphic designers.

## Service lines (as sold)

- Strategy development — Marketing Action Plan (the deliverable this skill produces)
- Digital media audit (audit-only, lighter than a full MAP)
- Brand & logo development / refresh (logo, mission/vision/values, style guides)
- Outsourced marketing retainers — tiered by monthly hours
- Website Optimisation Project — copy/SEO-led (not a redesign/dev service)
- Paid media management — social ads, Google Ads (excl. ad spend, **3-month minimum
  engagement**)
- SEO & GEO — traditional SEO plus generative-engine/AI-search optimisation
- Social media management — tiered content/community packages
- Sponsorship management, event management, new product development, project
  management, surveys, grant writing

## Known pricing inconsistencies (as of the Feb 2026 proposal template)

The template proposal had contradictory figures in multiple places (e.g. Marketing
Action Plan priced at both $10,500 and $2,400; retainer tiers named identically with
different hours/prices on different pages). **Do not hardcode specific dollar figures
into a MAP or reuse them as fact** — a MAP is a strategic document for the *client's*
marketing, not a Purple Giraffe sales proposal, and in both real MAPs read, Purple
Giraffe's own service pricing was never included in the document. If a generated MAP
ever needs to reference cost (e.g. "install a review widget, approx. cost X"), that's
a third-party tool cost, not a PG service fee — verify current PG service pricing with
a human before ever including it.

## Client-facing commercial constraints to respect in any recommendation

- Paid ads, social, and SEO retainers carry a 3-month minimum engagement — don't
  recommend a "quick 4-week paid campaign" without noting this.
  - Ad spend is never included in PG's paid media management fee — always separate the
    two when recommending paid campaigns.
- Client is expected to supply: T&Cs/privacy policy copy, brand assets (logos, images,
  video) unless a brand/content project is separately scoped.
- Billing is staged-by-project-milestone for one-off projects, monthly-in-arrears for
  retainers.

## Capability gaps identified vs. the installed skill set (worth flagging to a human,
not silently "fixed" inside a generated MAP)

CRO/conversion optimisation, pricing strategy, and standalone competitor research
(quarterly "competitor pulse") are not sold as their own product lines today, even
though the installed skill set (`cro`, `ab-testing`, `pricing`, `competitor-profiling`)
supports them well. If a MAP's audit surfaces a strong CRO or pricing-strategy
opportunity for a client, it's fine to recommend it as a *strategic priority* — just
don't imply it's an existing, off-the-shelf Purple Giraffe retainer inclusion unless
you've confirmed that with a human.

## Template hygiene (carried over from the original sales proposal review)

- Never leave a previous client's name/contact/data in a new MAP — check every table,
  example, and callout box before delivery.
- Never leave "Change Name", "xx", "TBC" placeholder text in a *final* delivered MAP —
  those are fine mid-draft, not at send.
- Cross-check every dollar figure and every percentage across the whole document for
  internal consistency before delivery — the source proposal template had multiple
  self-contradicting price/fee tables from drift over time; don't repeat that failure
  mode in generated MAPs.
