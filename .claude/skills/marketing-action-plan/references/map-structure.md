# Marketing Action Plan (MAP) structure

Source of truth: Purple Giraffe's actual master MAP template (`assets/PG-MAP-reference-template.docx`),
including Lynda Schenk's (founder) inline instructional comments, plus a recorded
walkthrough of how consultants actually build a MAP. This supersedes any
structure inferred from individual delivered MAPs — those are real examples of
this template applied to specific clients, but this file is the canonical shape.

## Build order (how consultants actually write it — not the final reading order)

Per Lynda's own instructions in the template: complete sections in this order,
not top-to-bottom as they appear in the final document:

1. Current Marketing Audit (all of it — traditional + digital)
2. About
3. Brand Strategy
4. Marketing Strategy
5. Marketing Activity Plan (the actions/calendar content)
6. Marketing Strategy on a page
7. Marketing Calendar
8. Marketing Roadmap
9. Contents (Table of Contents — done last, once headings are final)

The reasoning: you can't write the strategy or the "on a page" executive summary
until the audit is done and gaps are known, and you can't finalize a TOC until
the document's actual heading set (post-deletions) is settled.

## The repeating unit ("Marketing audit, strategy and actions")

Every topic in the audit section follows: `[Topic]` (PGHeading2) → **Audit**
(PGHeading3, current-state only — what's true right now) → **Strategy**
(PGHeading3, what direction to take — only where the topic warrants a
strategic statement beyond direct actions) → **`[Topic] actions`** (light-grey
callout box, PGAction heading + PGActionbullets, `#948794` mauve text — the
only place concrete, assignable tasks appear). Every action bullet must also
appear in the Marketing and communication calendar of activities.

Some short/factual topics go straight from Audit to Actions with no Strategy
paragraph — that's fine, don't force one.

## Full document skeleton (exact order, with delete-if-not-applicable flagged)

| Section | Delete? | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Always | Business name + subsidiary brand/s, industry, comparison framing |
| Business objective and commercial alignment | Always | Insert the actual business objectives — must come from client discovery, never invented |
| Marketing strategy on a page | Always | 3×3 grid: Audience/Strategies/Activities × Target market/Marketing goals/Marketing channels, Channels/Key strategies/Tactics&activities, Value proposition/Pricing&positioning/Measures of success |
| Marketing strategy roadmap | Always | Q1–Q4 wave graphic, Goal + Steps per quarter |
| About → Company history | Always | Dot points, historical order. Position/status/ethos within industry, desired direction |
| About → Industry research and insights | Always | Client-facing summary only — full research goes in Appendix 1 |
| Competitive summary | Always | **Top 3 competitors only.** Detailed tables (Competitor Brand Analysis, Competitor Social Media Review, Competitor Website Analysis) go in Appendix 2 |
| SWOT analysis | Always | Do this *after* research is complete — it should already be evident from the document, no separate research needed. Identify from the customer's point of view where possible |
| Key performance indicators | Always | Desired results must have a timeframe and be achievable |
| Marketing investment | Conditional | **Only include if the client is engaging Purple Giraffe on an ongoing basis** — the outsourced marketing fee covers the PG retainer line; add budget estimates for other out-of-pocket items (website, logo, etc.) per section. Remove entirely for audit-only or one-off engagements |
| Target market | Always | 6-segment wheel per identified market (repeat the wheel for each). Note underutilised segments and whether targeting should shift |
| Audience profiling (avatars) | Optional | "Meet [Name]" persona cards — optional extra, not a MAP requirement. If used, write fresh personas for this client — do not reuse example personas from other plans |
| What we want people to think/say/do | Optional | Optional extra — delete entirely if not wanted, not a MAP requirement |
| Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) factors | Conditional | Delete if irrelevant. 3–5 points per factor (Environmental/Social/Governance) |
| Social enterprise and impact-led marketing | Conditional | Rare — only for social enterprises, not-for-profits, purpose-led brands, or businesses with a strong community/impact narrative |
| Accessibility | Conditional | Newer section — include when appropriate to client/industry |
| Diversity, equity and inclusion | Conditional | Newer section — include when appropriate to client/industry |
| Products/services portfolio and pricing | Always | List all products/services + pricing; actions = pricing issues, product gaps, missing service terms (T&Cs, freight policy, etc.) |
| Sales channels and distribution (+ Sales channel opportunities) | Always | B2B/D2C channel types, current vs. desired % of sales by channel, growth opportunities |
| Customer journey | Always | Awareness/Findability/Reputation/Conversion/Advocacy touchpoint table — **not** a full journey-mapping exercise, just ensuring every touchpoint is considered. All touchpoints identified here should be addressed in the audit/strategy section below |
| **Marketing audit, strategy and actions** | Always (topics conditional) | See topic list below |
| Digital media (Website onward) | Always (sub-topics conditional) | See topic list below |
| Marketing and communication calendar of activities | Always | Element / Activity / Who / Qtr1–4 (shaded cells), summarizing every action from the whole document |
| Appendix 1: Industry research | Always | Full research, cited |
| Appendix 2: Competitor review + Competitor website analysis | Always | Detailed tables backing the Competitive summary |

## Topic list — "Marketing audit, strategy and actions" (non-digital)

Branding and positioning (→ Brand association prism [Animal/Car/Celebrity/
City-place/Font/Clothing Line — this is the client's **aspirational** brand in
2–5 years, not current state] → Positioning [Who/Where/What/Why/How] →
Mission → Vision → Values → Unique selling proposition → Point of difference)
→ Corporate assets (letterhead, business cards, presentation templates,
brochures, invoices, quotes) → Packaging/offering → **Industry awards**
(conditional) → **Tourism marketing** (conditional; if kept, break into
Accommodation marketing / Restaurant marketing / Commissionable experiences
as sub-topics per client type) → **Physical experience** (conditional;
premises, signage, shopfront, events held, winery if applicable) → **Stock
management** (conditional) → **Loyalty program strategy** (conditional) →
**Wine club** (conditional, wine industry only) → Traditional advertising
(print, radio, billboard, brochures, TV/video, signage, direct mail,
telemarketing, guerrilla marketing, merchandise) → **Public relations**
(conditional) → **Influencers** (conditional) → **Sponsorships and
associations** (conditional) → **Business/consumer events and trade show
attendance** (conditional) → Customer service (channels, complaint
resolution, staffing, feedback collection) → Other (catch-all for anything
not covered above)

## Topic list — "Digital media"

Website (Site audit checklist — see below — then desktop + mobile per-page
audit tables, then Images, then Strategy/KPIs) → SEO content (keyword in
title/H1, meta title/description optimised, header structure, image alt
text, backlinks, no broken links) → GEO content (GEO content checklist: case
studies, authoritative content, testimonials, optimised FAQs, alt text/meta,
content optimised for questions, cross-platform consistency, full service
detail, image quality, structured content — plus Site Audit health/crawled
pages/thematic reports/top issues from SEMrush) → **Search engine marketing
(SEM)** (conditional — only if client actively runs SEM) → **eNewsletter and
email direct marketing (eDM)** (conditional — only if client is doing EDM
activity) → **Google Business Profile** (conditional — nearly every business
should have one; delete only for the rare exception) → Citation management →
Reviews (per-location KPI table: current/desired/industry-standard) →
**Third-party online platforms** (conditional — Trip Advisor, Expedia, Airbnb,
wedding platforms, local/tourism/industry-specific sites — only if
applicable) → Social media marketing (platform review across Facebook/
Instagram/X/LinkedIn/YouTube/Other — see `references/social-media-checklists.md`
for exact per-platform checklists — then Existing content themes, Current
social engagement and community management, Current posting activity,
Current tone of voice, Current hashtags/emojis — this is **audit only**, no
strategy yet; then **Paid social media activity** [conditional] → Social
media objectives → Content pillars → Posting strategy → Tone of voice →
Emojis [optional table] → Hashtags [Branded/Geographic/Topical/Community/
Campaign] → Social engagement and community management → Paid social media
activity → **Events advertising** [conditional] → Negative feedback
management [action = write a negative-feedback policy if none exists]) →
Images and video → Analytics (recommend a third-party consolidation
dashboard via platform APIs over per-platform reporting)

## Website site audit checklist (desktop + mobile, separately)

Pages to audit: Homepage, About Us, Leadership/team, Products/Services,
Pricing, Resources, Contact, Testimonials/Success, Blog home, Sample blog
post, Sample landing page, Careers, Gallery/portfolio, Press, Privacy Policy,
Terms and Conditions, 404 page, Search results, Navigation menu, Footer.

Per page, check: clear page title, clear meta title, clear meta description,
spelling/grammar, no broken links, has CTA, images OK, info accurate, popups/
chat display correctly, inclusive, on-brand, notes/action items.

Whole-site checklist: industry/offering immediately clear, navigation
intuitive, CTA every page, users find key info quickly, meta data on every
page, page titles correct, business info/photos accurate and current,
inclusive and diverse, correct spelling/grammar, consistent design, working
internal links, readable/friendly copy, popups/chat clear, working footer
links with social icons, optimised FAQ section, populated case studies/
testimonials, copy optimised to answer specific questions (GEO), Google
Business Profile properly detailed, populated alt text and meta descriptions
site-wide.

## What NOT to do

- Don't invent competitors — the client identifies them; PG reviews their
  public marketing (website, social, ads if findable, annual reports).
- Don't invent industry research, metrics, or KPI baselines — missing data is
  "baseline to be established," not a filled-in guess.
- Don't include every conditional section for every client. Delete
  irrelevant sections **with tracked changes on**, so deletions are visible
  for review, not silently removed.
- Don't leave any blue instructional text or Word comments in a document
  being delivered to a client — see `SKILL.md` Step 5 (this is Purple
  Giraffe's own stated #1 rule for using this template).
- Don't add an action to a callout box without also adding it to the
  calendar, or vice versa.
